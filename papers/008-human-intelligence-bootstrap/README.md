# ARIS4C008 · The Human Intelligence Bootstrap

**Status:** Pilot 9 deep coding · all 50 Tier-1 additions first-pass complete; retained29 backfill and new50 second pass active

## Canonical question

> Which conditions, singly or in combination, are necessary, sufficient, enabling, or bottleneck conditions for the evolution of open-ended cumulative intelligence, and which nonhuman lineages already possess substantial subsets of those conditions?

The project begins from a simple "skill allocation" intuition: species differ dramatically in mobility, strength, lifespan, manipulation, cognition, sociality, communication, reproduction and energetic strategy. The scientific target is not to rank animals. It is to ask why many components associated with human intelligence evolved repeatedly in isolation, while their human combination appears exceptional.

## Target phenomenon

"Intelligence" is too broad to serve as a single endpoint. ARIS4C008 therefore distinguishes at least five partially separable outcomes:

1. individual innovation and flexible problem solving;
2. social acquisition of information and stable behavioural traditions;
3. cumulative improvement or acquisition of behaviours difficult to re-innovate individually;
4. cross-domain recombination, specialization and durable externalization of knowledge;
5. open-ended cumulative intelligence: a cultural system capable of repeatedly expanding the scope and complexity of inherited solutions.

These are not assumed to form a strict one-dimensional ladder. The primary analysis treats them as separate outcomes and only secondarily tests ordered-transition models.

## Central hypothesis: a bootstrap, not one magic trait

The working model is that the human transition was conjunctive rather than additive. Candidate modules are:

- generative cognition;
- high-capacity social transmission;
- communication;
- manipulation / embodiment;
- prolonged learning window and life history;
- energetic support;
- cooperative, connected social networks;
- durable externalization and environmental modification.

The key test is whether a weakest-link or multiplicative model explains advanced cultural outcomes better than a simple sum of "intelligence points".

A schematic contrast is:

Additive model: Y ~ C + T + M + L + E + S + X

Bootstrap model: Y ~ f(C × T × M × L × E × S × X)

where the symbols denote module scores, not assumed biological constants.

## Why necessary/sufficient claims are difficult

For fully open-ended human culture there is effectively one extant positive lineage. A species-level binary regression of "human versus nonhuman" cannot establish necessity or sufficiency.

The project instead triangulates evidence from:

- repeated lower-level transitions across many species;
- distantly related convergent high-cognition lineages;
- dissociation cases that possess a proposed cause without the target outcome;
- hominin archaeological time slices, used with explicit uncertainty rather than treated as independent species;
- phylogenetic models that account for shared ancestry.

A candidate condition is weakened as "sufficient" when it repeatedly occurs without the target transition. It is weakened as "necessary" when the transition occurs without it. Strong claims require replication across independent evolutionary origins or time-resolved evidence.

## High-value dissociation cases

- **Chimpanzees / bonobos:** rich cognition, tools, social learning and traditions; crucial near-human contrast.
- **Corvids:** convergent complex cognition and tool use on a very different neural architecture.
- **Parrots:** vocal learning, long lifespan, sociality and dexterous beak-foot manipulation.
- **Cetaceans:** large brains, social learning, vocal traditions and cultural repertoires, but very different manipulation and material persistence constraints.
- **Elephants:** long life, long development, social memory and cooperation.
- **Octopuses:** flexible cognition and manipulation with radically different social and life-history architecture.
- **Bumblebees / honeybees:** powerful dissociation controls for social learning and collective information despite tiny nervous systems.
- **Pigeons:** useful experimental evidence for cumulative route improvement.
- **Meerkats and cooperative breeders:** teaching / social support without human-like open-ended culture.

"Potential" in this project never means that a lineage is predicted to evolve into a human-like species. It means overlap with a hypothesized bootstrap configuration, with explicit missing modules and uncertainty.

## Main falsifiable hypotheses

### H1 · No single-trait sufficiency
Brain size, encephalization, tool use, vocal learning, social learning, long life, or group size alone will not be sufficient for high-level cumulative outcomes.

### H2 · Weakest-link / conjunction
For advanced cumulative outcomes, the minimum or interaction structure across key modules will predict outcomes better than the additive sum alone.

### H3 · Network-memory hypothesis
Social-network connectivity, tolerance, cross-group exchange and specialization will explain cultural breadth beyond census group size.

### H4 · Embodiment / externalization bottleneck
The translation of cognition into technological cumulative culture will depend on manipulatory affordances and the ability to create persistent environmental or material traces.

### H5 · Learning-window × energy interaction
Long juvenile learning periods and long lives will be most useful when sufficient energetic and social support permits costly brains and prolonged dependence.

### H6 · Open-endedness is the rarer transition
Minimal culture, cumulative change, and socially acquired supra-individual solutions will occur outside humans; the strongest discontinuity will lie in breadth, recombination and open-ended expansion.

## Interpretation boundary

The project should not state that any living animal is "on the way to becoming human". Evolution has no predetermined endpoint. Similarity is defined as similarity in measured causal modules, not as a ladder of biological worth or destiny.
