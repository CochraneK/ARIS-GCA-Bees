# Outcome codebook · ARIS4C008

**Status:** v0.1 frozen for pilot use. Changes after pilot data inspection must be logged.

The project does not use a single subjective "intelligence score". Outcomes are coded separately so that proposed causes are not smuggled into the definition of the target.

## O1 · Individual generative problem solving

Evidence that individuals can generate a novel or flexible solution without copying the specific solution from another individual.

Examples of admissible evidence:
- innovation in a novel task;
- transfer across changed task conditions;
- multi-step means-end solutions;
- reversal / detour / planning paradigms where sensory and motor demands are appropriate.

Not sufficient:
- brain size alone;
- one trained discrimination task;
- performance that can be explained entirely by prior shaping without generalization.

## O2 · Stable socially transmitted tradition

A behaviour or information pattern is socially acquired and persists within a group or population beyond a single dyad or demonstration episode.

Required coding fields:
- social-learning evidence;
- persistence duration;
- number of individuals/groups;
- wild versus captive;
- alternative ecological/genetic explanations.

## O3 · Supra-individual acquisition / cumulative improvement

At least one of:
1. later learners acquire a solution that naive individuals repeatedly fail to generate independently under matched conditions; or
2. a sequence of socially transmitted modifications yields improved performance or complexity relative to earlier states.

This level deliberately includes nonhuman cases.

## O4 · Cultural repertoire breadth

Number and diversity of independently evidenced socially transmitted behavioural domains, corrected where possible for:
- research effort;
- number of populations studied;
- observation duration;
- ecological opportunity.

Use domain breadth rather than raw behaviour counts as the main variable. **Raw ACDB row counts are prohibited as the primary O4 outcome**: Pilot 4 showed that the current ACDB release is a curated case database rather than an exhaustive species repertoire census. ACDB can support presence, domain and transmission coding, but repertoire breadth requires a dedicated systematic coding protocol.

## O5 · Recombination, specialization and distributed knowledge

Evidence that cultural performance depends on more than copying one complete solution.

Candidate indicators:
- recombination of components learned from different sources;
- complementary specialization across individuals;
- division of cognitive/cultural labour;
- cross-group import of solutions;
- network-level retention of knowledge not held by every individual.

## O6 · Persistent externalization

Information or functional structure persists outside the nervous system in artifacts or modified environments and can influence later behaviour.

Candidate manifestations:
- durable tools or tool kits;
- persistent constructions;
- caches, trails, modified sites or other recoverable environmental traces;
- representational or symbolic storage where supported.

This outcome is graded; it is not defined as "writing".

## O7 · Open-ended cumulative intelligence

Operational target for the strongest human-like transition.

A taxon/time slice can score high only if evidence supports all of the following dimensions rather than one spectacular behaviour:
- repeated cumulative change across multiple domains;
- recombination of inherited components;
- expansion of solution space rather than convergence to one fixed optimum;
- persistence across generations;
- capacity for specialization / distributed knowledge;
- substantial externalization or durable modification of the environment.

For the pilot, O7 is treated as a latent/graded construct with uncertainty, not a binary "human = 1" variable.

## Evidence states

Every outcome observation uses one of:
- positive;
- experimentally tested negative / failed;
- not tested;
- ambiguous;
- disputed.

**Missing is never coded as zero.**

## Confidence

Each coded claim receives:
- source count;
- evidence type;
- wild/captive context;
- replication status where known;
- confidence: low / medium / high;
- notes on sensory, motor, ecological and motivational comparability.

## Anti-anthropocentrism rule

A species is not penalized because it cannot perform a human-designed task using a human-like body. Task accessibility and ecological validity are coded separately from inferred cognitive capacity.
