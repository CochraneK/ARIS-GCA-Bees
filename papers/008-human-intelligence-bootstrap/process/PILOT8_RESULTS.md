# Pilot 8 results · Configuration-diverse screening expansion

## What Pilot 8 changed

Pilot 8 replaced the original “smart-animal panel” logic with a staged information-design workflow.

### Broad pool
A 238-operational-taxon screening pool now combines:
- the retained 29;
- ACDB/ASNR candidates;
- matched ordinary bird/mammal controls;
- underrepresented-clade controls;
- explicit theory-discriminating candidates.

### Standardized low-cost screening
The pool can be rebuilt against AnAge, AnimalTraits, EltonTraits, PanTHERIA and ASNR. These variables guide search allocation and configuration diversity but do not become global intelligence scores.

### Tier-1 wave
Fifty new taxa were selected for deep coding, creating a planned 79-taxon panel. Selection combines mandatory theory/clade strata with biological-proxy novelty and source feasibility.

### Counterexample evidence
Fourteen theory candidates now have an exact-species evidence seed spanning planning, tool use, construction, vocal learning, social transmission, cooperation, collective decision-making and externalized infrastructure.

### Taxonomy
All 238 operational taxa have an explicit taxonomy status/crosswalk. One current combination is absent from OpenTree exact TNRS and is handled with a documented legacy OTT crosswalk.

## Critical methodological discovery

The first Tier-1 recoverability run exposed a simulation-design artifact: a flexible static “interaction” model was able to absorb weakest-link and threshold outcomes. Pilot 8 therefore separates:
- the primary equal-complexity static architecture gate;
- dynamic feedback/network hypotheses requiring temporal structure.

This correction materially improves interpretability and is now part of the canonical research plan.

## Current conclusion

Pilot 8 does **not** identify the necessary/sufficient conditions for human intelligence.

It establishes that:
- the 29-taxon seed was too narrow;
- the 238-taxon screening framework can generate ordinary and dissociative counterexamples;
- a staged 79-taxon panel is plausible in principle;
- A–F evidence and O1–O7 outcomes remain the main deep-coding task;
- H/J missingness should be improved;
- configuration geometry matters even after sample-size expansion.

This is sufficient to close Pilot 8 as a sampling-and-identifiability milestone, but not to open confirmatory inference.
