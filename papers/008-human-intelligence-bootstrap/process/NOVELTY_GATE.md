# Novelty gate assessment · ARIS4C008

**Status:** PROVISIONAL PASS  
**Date:** 2026-09-18

## Why the original novelty claim had to change

Several components initially treated as potentially novel already have strong precedents.

- Laland & Seed (2021) already frame human cognitive uniqueness as interactions and reinforcing feedbacks among multiple domains.
- Mesoudi & Thornton (2018) already use “necessary and sufficient” language for criteria defining cumulative cultural evolution.
- Dean et al. (2012) already link cumulative-task success to a package of teaching, imitation and prosociality.
- Reader & Laland (2002) already combine behavioral-flexibility counts with phylogeny and research-effort correction.
- Reader et al. (2011) already derive a cross-species primate general/cultural-intelligence composite.
- Street et al. (2017) already test coevolution among social learning, brain size, lifespan and sociality using phylogenetic comparative methods and research-effort correction.
- Migliano & Vinicius (2022) already propose network memory and multilevel sociality.
- Morgan & Feldman (2025) already make open-endedness the key proposed human distinction.

Therefore 008 cannot be sold as “many traits interact,” “life history matters,” “network memory matters,” or “open-endedness matters.”

## Narrowed novelty

008 is provisionally novel in the **combination of design commitments**:

1. multi-clade rather than primate-only comparative sampling;
2. source-level exact-species evidence and explicit exemplar separation;
3. decomposed outcome levels instead of human = 1 / animal = 0;
4. deliberate counterexample sampling to attack necessity and sufficiency;
5. explicit model competition:
   - additive;
   - weakest-link;
   - threshold;
   - interaction/feedback;
6. research-effort and phylogenetic bias controls;
7. missing-not-at-random handling;
8. a separate hominin time layer for event ordering.

## Strongest testable novelty sentence

> We test whether increasingly open-ended cumulative intelligence is associated with a quantitatively distinguishable **configuration architecture**, rather than simply higher scores on a general cognitive/cultural factor.

This directly separates 008 from the primate-g literature and from conceptual feedback accounts.

## Remaining risk

The novelty pass is provisional until:
- broader citation-chaining is completed;
- the systematic search includes non-English/indexing variants where feasible;
- the final outcome codebook is fixed;
- a prior study using explicit configurational/necessary-condition methods in comparative cognition is ruled out more systematically.

The manuscript should present novelty as a **design and synthesis advance**, not as discovery of previously unknown candidate traits.
