# Theory-candidate exact-species evidence seed · Pilot 8

This seed converts the 14 theory-discriminating candidates from conceptual examples into source-traceable module evidence.

## Coding rules

- `positive` means the cited exact-species study directly supports the narrow trait named.
- `positive_proxy` means the study supports a narrower cognitive proxy but **must not** be promoted to the full A-module construct.
- `positive_with_scope_limit` is used where the biological phenomenon is clear but the source is not itself a behavioral mechanism experiment.
- No absence of a paper is coded as a biological negative.
- No component is called sufficient for open-ended cumulative intelligence merely because it is spectacular.

## Main dissociations now represented

- **A without assumed human-like culture:** scrub-jay future planning; giant-tortoise learning/memory; Portia tactical flexibility; cleaner-wrasse social cognition proxy.
- **B/C without open-endedness assumed:** zebra-finch song culture; sac-winged-bat vocal imitation; naked-mole-rat vocal dialect; cleaner-fish social learning; rat social transmission.
- **D without open-ended technological accumulation assumed:** habitual stone-tool use in sea otters.
- **E without human-like cognition assumed:** beaver dams, satin-bowerbird bowers, leaf-cutter-ant fungus cultivation.
- **F without precision manipulation assumed:** wolves, African wild dogs, naked mole-rats, leaf-cutter ants.

These are candidate **anti-sufficiency contrasts**, not proof that the focal component is insufficient. Formal insufficiency requires an independently coded outcome state and adequate detection/effort controls.
