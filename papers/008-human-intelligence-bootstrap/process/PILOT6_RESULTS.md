# PILOT 6 RESULTS · ARIS4C008

Pilot 6 adds two independent falsification layers that were missing from the original cross-species design.

## Hominin temporal triangulation

The archaeological layer now codes 15 first-secure-evidence intervals and 14 explicit tests of temporal prerequisite claims.

Eleven tests directly compare two dated milestone intervals. An automated interval-direction audit confirmed all eleven coded contradictions: even the youngest plausible age of the target is older than the oldest plausible age of the candidate prerequisite.

This produces strong rejections of narrowly defined observable prerequisites while preserving the distinction between "not yet archaeologically visible" and "biologically absent."

## Neural architecture

The neural seed contains region- and method-specific observations rather than a global intelligence score.

Key dissociations:
- elephant: ~257B total neurons but ~5.6B cortical neurons;
- human: ~86.1B total and ~16.3B cortical neurons;
- parrots/corvids: high pallial neuron counts in small brains;
- honeybee: ~1M brain neurons despite rich learning/social behavior;
- octopus: large fraction of neurons distributed in arms;
- northern minke whale method calibrator: ~12.8B cortical neurons by older stereology versus ~3.2B by isotropic fractionator.

The architecture itself—not just total neural quantity—therefore becomes a candidate condition family.

## Design consequence

008 now has three distinct falsification directions:

1. **Cross-taxon dissociation:** X exists without Y, or Y without X.
2. **Hominin temporal contradiction:** Y securely appears before proposed prerequisite X.
3. **Measurement-architecture contradiction:** apparently similar "brain size/neuron count" values encode biologically different computation across clades/methods.

No single route proves causality, but agreement across them can sharply narrow plausible necessary-condition sets.
