## What this project is

**The Human Intelligence Bootstrap: Necessary, Sufficient, and Bottleneck Configurations for Open-Ended Cumulative Intelligence Across Animals**

A comparative, phylogenetically informed research program testing which combinations of cognition, social transmission, embodiment, life history, energy, communication, social networks, and externalized information are associated with transitions from animal social learning to open-ended cumulative intelligence, using extant taxa, dissociation cases, and hominin time slices.
