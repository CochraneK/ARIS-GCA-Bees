# ARIS4C002 · Agent takeover brief

## What this project is

**Testing the periodic-table hypothesis of human language: predictive evidence does not support a global circular organization**

A predictive stress test of the simple global-circle form of the language periodic-table hypothesis across TLI, GBI, and WALS. Family-held-out evidence and direct circularity diagnostics do not support the tested global circular geometry; hierarchical/non-circular models provide stronger predictive benchmarks without establishing one universal tree geometry.
