# ARIS4C009 · Research context

## Project

**The Fidelity Frontier in Computational Psychiatry: Decomposing Acquisition and Encoding Loss in Psychopathology**

## Compact scope

A measurement framework that separates psychiatric acquisition from encoding loss. Pilot-0 runs on open DAIS-C schizophrenia interview material with privacy-preserving source engineering, structural segmentation, blinded multi-model AI boundary calibration, and later same-source representation fidelity benchmarking.
