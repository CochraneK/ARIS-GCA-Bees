# ARIS4C009 Pilot-0 · DAIS-C interpretation limits

## Purpose

DAIS-C is used to calibrate the **measurement machinery**, not to test a disease-effect hypothesis.

## Non-negotiable limitations

### 1. The source interview was not designed for EASE/EAWE/STEP

DAIS-C interviews focused on linguistic creativity, abstract ideas and related tasks.

Therefore:

- R2 in Pilot-0 is **phenomenology-informed R2-lite**;
- no EASE score is inferred;
- absence of a self-disorder relation is not evidence of absence in the participant.

### 2. Clinical and comparison interviewing is not exchangeable

Published corpus characteristics show substantial differences in interview-only status and topic breadth between groups.

Therefore Pilot-0 does **not** interpret:

- episode count differences;
- response length differences;
- interviewer-word differences;
- topic richness differences;

as schizophrenia effects.

Cohort labels are used only for structural coverage checks.

### 3. Recruitment N is not analyzable-source N

The resource describes 15 clinical and 14 comparison speakers.

The public full-interaction archive layer currently yields 15 clinical and 13 comparison transcripts under the structural parser.

The fidelity benchmark uses only records that satisfy the source-integrity gate.

### 4. Turn boundaries are not phenomenological boundaries

An interviewer-response pair is only a microepisode.

The observed Pilot-0 distribution contains many extremely short responses, so multi-turn structural windows are being evaluated before human calibration.

### 5. Public availability does not erase privacy concerns

ARIS4C does not mirror raw transcripts.

Public repository outputs are limited to:

- archive checksum;
- aggregate file inventory;
- aggregate structural counts;
- aggregate episode/window distributions;
- analysis code.

## Permitted Pilot-0 conclusion

A successful Pilot-0 can establish that:

> the ARIS4C009 acquisition/encoding measurement pipeline can be applied reproducibly to real schizophrenia interview material under explicit source, privacy and segmentation constraints.

It cannot establish clinical superiority of one assessment method or a complete phenomenological model of schizophrenia.
