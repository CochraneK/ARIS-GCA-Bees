## P1

---

## P2

## P3

**INTERVIEWER:** What did you mean when you said the room felt different?  
**PARTICIPANT:** I knew it was the same room, but it felt oddly unfamiliar. I can't really explain it better than that.  
**INTERVIEWER:** Were you saying you literally thought it had been replaced?  
**PARTICIPANT:** No. I knew it hadn't. It was more the feeling of it.

## P4

**INTERVIEWER:** How had you been sleeping that week?  
**PARTICIPANT:** About four hours a night, maybe five. I was waking early.  
**INTERVIEWER:** Did that change how tired you felt in the afternoon?  
**PARTICIPANT:** Definitely. I kept falling asleep on the bus.  
**INTERVIEWER:** What music did you listen to when you were a child?  
**PARTICIPANT:** Mostly old pop songs my parents liked.

## P5

## P6

## P7

**INTERVIEWER:** What happened after you heard the noise?  
**PARTICIPANT:** I checked the window, then went into the hall, then called my sister. She said she hadn't heard anything. I stayed awake for another hour because I was still thinking about it.

---

## P8

**INTERVIEWER:** Did the thought feel like something you chose to think?  
**PARTICIPANT:** At first I would have said no, but that's not quite right. I could stop thinking about it if I focused on something else. It was more that it arrived before I wanted it to.
