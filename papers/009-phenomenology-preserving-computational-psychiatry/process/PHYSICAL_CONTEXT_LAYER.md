# ARIS4C009 · Physical-context constraint layer

## Purpose

The phrase "preserve reality" can be misleading in psychiatry unless different kinds of reality claims are separated.

ARIS4C009 distinguishes:

1. **physical/contextual facts** that may be independently constrained;
2. **behavioral facts** that may be externally observed;
3. **first-person experiential structure** that requires first-person/second-person evidence;
4. **clinical interpretation**;
5. **computational latent variables**.

No layer automatically adjudicates the others.

## Context state

For time (t), define:

[
W_t=(T_t,L_t,A_t,S_t,Sl_t,M_t,P_t,ldots)
]

where candidate elements include:

- (T): time;
- (L): coarse location;
- (A): activity;
- (S): social context;
- (Sl): sleep/wake;
- (M): medication timing;
- (P): optional physiology.

## Examples of claims that can be externally constrained

- the participant was at home rather than at work;
- the episode occurred after a poor-sleep night;
- another person was physically present;
- heart rate was elevated;
- the phone recorded low mobility.

## Examples that physical data cannot settle by itself

- whether a thought felt "mine";
- whether an action felt self-authored;
- whether the environment felt unreal;
- whether an event felt personally salient;
- whether a voice was experienced as internal/external in the participant's phenomenological sense;
- what an event meant to the participant.

## Separate validity channels

Report:

[
F_{physical}
]

for factual/context reconstruction where independent anchors exist,

and separately:

[
F_{phenomenological}
]

for source-grounded experiential reconstruction.

Also keep:

[
V_{behavioral}, V_{neural}, U_{prediction}
]

separate.

## Why this matters

A model can be:

- physically well anchored but phenomenologically poor;
- phenomenologically faithful but biologically uninformative;
- predictively strong but semantically distorted.

The ARIS4C009 objective is not to force these dimensions into one number.

## Privacy constraint

Physical-context anchoring can become surveillance.

Default hierarchy:

1. use the least identifying anchor that answers the research question;
2. prefer coarse categories over precise raw traces;
3. do not collect passive streams "just in case";
4. retain raw identifying sensor data only when scientifically necessary and ethically approved;
5. treat privacy exposure as part of the burden/cost vector.

## Longer-term model

A later multimodal state-space model can use:

[
P(s_t|X^{phen}_t,X^{beh}_t,W_t,X^{bio}_t)
]

but (s_t) is a model-dependent latent state, not a discovered physical essence of the person.

Competing state definitions and models must remain possible.
