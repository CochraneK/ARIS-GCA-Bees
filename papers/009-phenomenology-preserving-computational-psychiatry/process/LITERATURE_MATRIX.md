## Evidence synthesis

The literature already establishes five facts important to 009:

1. psychiatric information is **elicited**, not simply read out from an internal state;
2. first-person fidelity is distinct from psychometric reliability and intended-use validity;
3. phenomenology can already be formalized computationally;
4. narrative/clinical compression can produce measurable omission and reframing;
5. computational psychiatry needs stronger ecological and psychometric validation.

The candidate gap is therefore not any one of these components. It is their integration into a **controlled two-stage experimental benchmark**:

[
H \rightarrow A_m \rightarrow X^{(m)} \rightarrow E_k \rightarrow Z^{(m,k)}
]

with fixed-source encoding tests, separate acquisition tests, independent reconstruction queries, burden/rate matching, and use-conditioned Pareto comparison.
