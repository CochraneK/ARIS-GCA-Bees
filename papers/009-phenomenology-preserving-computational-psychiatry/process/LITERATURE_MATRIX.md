## Evidence pattern

The literature already demonstrates:

1. rich first-person structure can be assessed reproducibly enough for empirical work;
2. some of that structure maps to behavior and neurophysiology;
3. computational psychiatry can formalize latent dynamics but has nontrivial reliability and ecological-validity problems;
4. self-report substitution can materially change what is being measured;
5. person-specific longitudinal dynamics contain information lost by diagnosis-level aggregation.

The missing methodological object is an explicit, source-grounded **loss function across psychiatric representations**.

## Search priorities for full ARIS run

- phenomenological fidelity / experience sampling / descriptive experience sampling;
- EASE, EAWE, STEP validation and inter-rater studies;
- self-report versus interview discrepancies;
- computational-assay reliability and parameter recovery;
- ecological validity of laboratory tasks;
- neurophenomenology across psychosis, depression, OCD, trauma and dissociation;
- state-space / dynamical systems models in psychiatry;
- rate-distortion and semantic compression methods;
- participant-centered validation of psychiatric representations;
- cross-cultural phenomenological measurement.
