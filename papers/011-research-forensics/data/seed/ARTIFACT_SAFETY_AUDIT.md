# Seed artifact-safety audit

This is a six-record source-anatomy pilot, not an estimate of historical-artifact availability in the literature.

## Current-artifact result

Among the six existing ARIS4C011 seed targets:
- three current representations are already known to be unusable for primary Track A because of explicit outcome/update contamination;
- three require case-level historical comparison before eligibility can be decided;
- zero are currently promoted as SAFE_EXACT solely on the basis of a present-day publisher/bibliographic representation.

This is expected and is the point of the gate: present-day metadata/full text is often not the same object that a pre-correction or pre-retraction detector would have seen.

## Important nuance

BLOCKED_CURRENT does not mean the paper can never enter Track A. It means the current representation is contaminated. A verified historical snapshot of the same published version can still become SAFE_EXACT.

UNKNOWN_CURRENT is intentionally conservative. It remains outside primary Track A until version equivalence is demonstrated.

## Next empirical task

Scale this audit to a stratified sample and estimate:
- exact historical artifact yield;
- proxy-only yield;
- contamination rate;
- missing-provenance rate;
- how these rates differ by year, publisher, field, and issue type.

Do not interpret those availability differences as misconduct differences.
