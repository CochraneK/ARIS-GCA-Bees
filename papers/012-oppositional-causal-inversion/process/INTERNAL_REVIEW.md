# INTERNAL REVIEW — ARIS4C012

Updated: 2026-09-18

## Overall assessment

The project is now stronger as a **construct-validation + evidence-map paper** than as a broad new causal theory.

The strongest current contribution is methodological:

> Can scientists distinguish, with prespecified rules and independent coding, a restricted class of causal claims in which an exposure produces a functional opposite after explicit index changes or feedback?

The paper should be rejected by us before submission if it cannot answer that question reliably.

## Major reviewer attack 1 — "This is just unintended consequences"

### Risk
Very high.

### Current defense
The manuscript now explicitly concedes that unintended-consequence theory, perverse effects, complex-system feedback, rebound, boomerang, and paradox literatures predate OCI.

### Required evidence
OCI must outperform the generic category by reliably distinguishing:
- functional-opposite effects;
- ordinary harms;
- trade-offs;
- paradoxical coexistence;
- null effects;
- reverse causality.

Without independent discriminant validity, rename OCI as a coding schema rather than a theoretical framework.

## Major reviewer attack 2 — "Opposite is subjective"

### Risk
Critical.

### Current defense
Functional opposition must be prespecified through established bipolar constructs, formal complements, domain theory, or independent coder agreement.

### Required evidence
Gate B reliability on `opposition_valid`.

If agreement <0.70:
- revise definition;
- repeat with a fresh sample;
- do not adjudicate the old sample into apparent success.

## Major reviewer attack 3 — "The mechanism taxonomy is arbitrary"

### Risk
High.

### Current defense
Eight mechanism families were specified before full evidence mapping.

### Required evidence
Independent mechanism coding plus empirical collapse/merge decisions.

Likely future simplification:
- actor/level/time/construct are **index-switch dimensions**;
- feedback/nonlinearity/filtering/power may be **mechanism dimensions**.

These may be better modeled as orthogonal axes than eight mutually exclusive classes.

This is an important possible redesign after Gate B.

## Major reviewer attack 4 — "You are pooling incomparable phenomena"

### Risk
High.

### Current defense
No grand OCI effect size is permitted.

Quantitative synthesis is allowed only within compatible families.

The primary cross-domain output is a taxonomy/evidence map, not a common causal magnitude.

## Major reviewer attack 5 — "Orwell is clickbait or ideological framing"

### Risk
High.

### Current defense
The slogans are explicitly a motivating stress test and are scientifically separated.

### Recommendation
Keep Orwell in the introduction but not in the title.

Do not structure the paper around three slogans. Structure it around:
1. representation;
2. opposition validity;
3. index dimensions;
4. mechanisms;
5. evidence map.

Use the slogans as one boxed example/figure.

## Major reviewer attack 6 — "Search strategy is biased toward named paradoxes"

### Risk
High.

### Current defense
The search includes broader terms such as backfire, counterproductive, iatrogenic, rebound, overload, and unintended consequence.

### Remaining problem
The v0.2 frame is uneven:
- information/public-information query: only 3;
- organization/control query: only 2;
- iatrogenic query: 20.

### Required fix
Targeted expansion after Gate B plus citation chasing and historical terminology.

Do not interpret raw family counts as phenomenon prevalence.

## Major reviewer attack 7 — "Single-coder development contaminated the ontology"

### Risk
Critical until Gate B passes.

### Current defense
Coder B materials were frozen before B labels and a blind packet was created.

### Current state
The canonical 012 Coder B file remains blank.

The user reports WorkBuddy has finished, but no ARIS4C012 WorkBuddy artifact is discoverable in:
- main;
- visible GitHub branches;
- current Project/Library files.

Therefore independent validation must remain unclaimed until the output is ingested.

## Major reviewer attack 8 — "A classifier that needs domain experts is not machine-codeable"

### Risk
Medium-high.

### Future test
After human construct validation:
1. create human gold labels;
2. train/rule-test simple lexical + metadata baseline;
3. compare an LLM structured coder;
4. report abstention and error types;
5. test on held-out domains.

Machine-codeability should be an empirical result, not a descriptive adjective.

## Major reviewer attack 9 — "Index restoration is notation, not science"

### Risk
High.

### Required value-add
Show at least one of:
- better inter-rater reliability than generic paradox labels;
- better distinction between true reversal and side effects;
- prediction of moderator/mechanism family on held-out cases;
- reduction in semantic contradictions;
- transfer across domains.

If index restoration only makes examples look cleaner, the contribution is pedagogical rather than theoretical.

## Major reviewer attack 10 — "The paper is too broad"

### Risk
Medium-high.

### Preferred first-paper scope
Theory + construct-validation Pilot + systematic evidence map.

Do not attempt:
- universal effect-size estimation;
- normative conclusions;
- causal mechanism proof for every domain;
- political generalization from censorship/deterrence cases.

## Current decision

**CONTINUE**, but only under a narrow claim.

### Promotion condition
The framework moves from provisional to validated only after independent Coder B reliability.

### Fallback if reliability fails
Publish a negative/diagnostic paper:

> Why Apparent Causal Paradoxes Resist a Unified Taxonomy: Evidence from Cross-Domain Double Coding

A failed unified ontology is scientifically interesting if the failure is transparent.
