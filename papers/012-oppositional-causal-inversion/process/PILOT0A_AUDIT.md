# PILOT 0A FEASIBILITY AUDIT — ARIS4C012

Updated: 2026-09-18

## Scope

This is **Coder A feasibility coding**, not the final independent double-coded Pilot 0.

Thirty records were stratified across six families:

1. conflict / security;
2. autonomy / choice;
3. information / attention;
4. boomerang / iatrogenic interventions;
5. economic / network rebound;
6. organizational / measurement paradoxes.

The purpose was to test whether the schema can reject tempting false positives and retain null/counterevidence before requesting an independent second coding pass.

## Coder A counts

### OCI candidacy
- yes: **21 / 30**
- no: **7 / 30**
- uncertain: **2 / 30**

### Functional-opposition validity
- yes: **21 / 30**
- no: **4 / 30**

These are feasibility labels from one coder and must not be interpreted as prevalence estimates.

## Most important result

The framework did **not** absorb every paper with "paradox", "backfire", or "ignorance" language.

Examples coded as non-OCI or uncertain include:
- broad organizational paradox theory, because contradictory/interdependent demands are not necessarily a directional X -> opposite-X relation;
- strategic paradox management, for the same reason;
- counterfinality, because detriment to an alternative goal increases perceived focal-goal instrumentality rather than producing the functional opposite of the exposure;
- diversionary-conflict work when the measured outcome is external force rather than a demonstrated domestic opposite state;
- rational inattention and deliberate ignorance when the work establishes selective attention/ignorance as functional but not a direct opposite-producing causal outcome.

This discrimination is necessary if OCI is to be more than a relabeling exercise.

## Deliberately retained counterevidence

The packet includes papers that weaken simple Orwellian readings:

- the 2010 choice-overload meta-analysis reports a near-zero mean effect with substantial heterogeneity;
- autonomy/self-determination meta-analyses generally associate autonomy-related constructs with adaptive outcomes;
- a 2026 information-avoidance study reports that information sharing increases awareness and prosocial choices;
- a 2014 analysis across four randomized alcohol-intervention samples found no boomerang effect among lighter drinkers.

Null or beneficial effects are not screening failures. They define boundary conditions.

## Triad diagnosis after Pilot 0A

### "War is peace"
**Exact slogan: weak formulation.**

Best scientific mappings are narrower:
- intergroup competition -> intragroup cooperation;
- credible threat/capability -> deterrence under strategic conditions;
- security-seeking -> insecurity through a security dilemma.

War, threat, competition, deterrence, cohesion, support, and peace must remain separate constructs.

### "Freedom is slavery"
**Moderately strong as a construct/time-switch family, weak as a universal claim.**

Best mappings:
- more options -> overload under complexity/difficulty/uncertainty moderators;
- short-run work flexibility -> collective availability norms -> reduced ability to disconnect.

Autonomy meta-analyses are essential counterevidence against a general negative freedom effect.

### "Ignorance is strength"
**Most heterogeneous and semantically dangerous.**

Best mappings are not generalized ignorance:
- selective filtering / less-is-more under ecological and bias-variance conditions;
- rational allocation of scarce attention;
- more public information -> lower welfare only under specific coordination/private-information models.

Externally imposed ignorance, censorship, deliberate ignorance, information avoidance, and adaptive filtering must not be collapsed.

## Strong benchmark instances beyond Orwell

The cross-domain framework is better stress-tested by:
- Braess paradox: added network capacity -> higher equilibrium travel cost under defined conditions;
- rebound/Jevons families: efficiency -> partially offset or sometimes reversed resource savings;
- social-norm boomerang: behavior-change message -> opposite behavior in a subgroup;
- iatrogenic intervention effects: treatment/prevention -> deterioration in some conditions;
- Goodhart-type feedback: optimization of a measure -> degradation of its validity;
- safe-development/levee feedback: protection -> development/exposure -> higher long-run risk.

These examples have clearer causal structures than the literary slogans and should anchor construct validation.
