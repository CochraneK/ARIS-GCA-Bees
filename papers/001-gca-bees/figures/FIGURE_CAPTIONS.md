# ARIS4C001 · Figure captions

## Figure 1. Published learning covariance
Descriptive Fisher-z synthesis of the three reported task-pair correlations across the visual and olfactory free-flying conditions in Finke et al. (2023). Points are pooled correlations and horizontal intervals are approximate 95% intervals. The synthesis contains only two related conditions and is **not** a population meta-analysis.

## Figure 2. Cross-modality factor loading structure
Published one-factor loadings reported by Peñaherrera-Aguirre et al. (2024) for visual and olfactory conditions. The relative loading profiles are nearly identical (Tucker congruence = 0.99996), while this statistical congruence does not establish a unitary biological mechanism.

## Figure 3. Synthetic model-recovery diagnostic
BIC model-selection rates across 200 synthetic replications at each sample size under a two-correlated-factor data-generating model (loading = 0.65; latent-factor correlation = 0.35). This is an experimental-design diagnostic only and **not empirical evidence about bees**.
