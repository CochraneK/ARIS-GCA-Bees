### Why this fit

The Comparative Psychology section explicitly covers animal cognition, learning, decision making, social learning, and related comparative mechanisms, and currently accepts **Hypothesis and Theory** articles. The article type is designed for a novel, testable interpretation or model built from recent findings and permits original analyses alongside theory.

Current journal constraints checked on 2026-09-18:

- Hypothesis and Theory maximum main-text length: 12,000 words.
- Frontiers requires 5–8 keywords.
- The manuscript source file and a PDF are required at submission.
- Data availability, funding, conflict-of-interest, authorship/contribution, and related statements are completed in the portal.
- Frontiers in Psychology A-type APC: **CHF 3,150 if accepted**. Institutional support or fee support may apply.

## Scope decision

The manuscript is submitted as a **Hypothesis and Theory** paper, not as a Systematic Review and not as Original Research.

Reason: the paper contains a transparent published-summary quantitative synthesis and a model-recovery diagnostic, but its central contribution is a falsifiable theoretical integration of learning covariance and uncertainty-sensitive choice. It does not claim a new animal experiment or a complete systematic-search corpus.
