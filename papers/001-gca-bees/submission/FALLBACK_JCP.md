# Fallback journal · Journal of Comparative Psychology (APA)

Use this route if Frontiers’ APC or article-positioning is undesirable.

Current submission requirements checked 2026-09-18:

- Microsoft Word (.docx) or LaTeX (.tex) as the source manuscript.
- PDF of the manuscript accompanies the submission.
- APA 7 style.
- Abstract maximum 250 words.
- Separate title page with author and affiliation.
- Author footnote/correspondence details.
- All pages numbered.
- Cumulative line numbers.
- If an animal name appears in the title, the scientific name should be included after the common name.
- Effect sizes and confidence intervals are expected.
- Up to three potential reviewers may be suggested.

Official instructions:
https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/com/submit

## Conversion notes

The scientific content should remain unchanged. For this fallback:

1. shorten the Frontiers abstract to <=250 words;
2. adapt title to include *Apis mellifera* if required by the editor’s reading of the animal-name rule;
3. convert references and headings to APA 7;
4. add cumulative line numbering to the Word/PDF submission copy;
5. move author/funding/acknowledgment information to APA’s required title-page/author-note structure.

This fallback is not currently the canonical formatted package; Frontiers Comparative Psychology is.
