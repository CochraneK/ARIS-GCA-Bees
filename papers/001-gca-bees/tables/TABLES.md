## Table 3. Competing models and decisive predictions

| Model | Core assumption | Evidence that would favor it | Strong falsifier / challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 · single general factor | Learning and uncertainty-sensitive indicators share one latent factor | Positive cross-domain covariance and superior held-out prediction from one factor | Reliable uncertainty variation but near-zero coupling to learning/GCA |
| M2 · two correlated factors | Learning factor G and uncertainty factor U are distinct but correlated | Stable non-zero rho(G,U) with better fit/prediction than M1/M3 | rho(G,U) near zero or one-factor model predicts equally well |
| M3 · two independent factors | Learning and uncertainty factors are separable | Reliable structure within domains but little cross-domain covariance | Stable cross-domain covariance not explained by nuisance variables |
| M4 · task-local associative | Opt-out follows learned stimulus/response values and reinforcement history | Associative model predicts trial-level opt-out and transfer as well as richer models | Generalization patterns not predicted by value history/similarity |
| M5 · hybrid | Stable individual differences coexist with associative trial-level choice | Both individual factor and associative history add held-out predictive value | One component adds no predictive information |

**Note.** Predictive-precision/confidence-like latent variables remain exploratory and are not privileged over these competitors.
