# ARIS4C015 · Provisional Pilot-M candidate cross-source validation

Updated: 2026-09-19

## Scope

This note separates two questions that must not be conflated:

1. **Bibliographic identity / later-use cross-check** — can the OpenAlex candidate be
   independently identified in publisher and non-OpenAlex sources, and is there
   evidence that later literature actually uses/cites it?
2. **Trajectory / Beauty-Coefficient validation** — does an independent
   bibliographic citation source reproduce the annual citation trajectory and
   support the OpenAlex-specific B classification?

Only question 1 is addressed here. Question 2 remains open.

## Candidate W2018826127

**OpenAlex candidate:** *An Experimental Study of the Reflection of X-Rays from
Calcite*.

Independent identity evidence:

- American Physical Society record: Bergen Davis and W. M. Stempel,
  *Physical Review* **17**, 608–623, published 1 May 1921,
  DOI `10.1103/PhysRev.17.608`.
  https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.17.608
- APS volume archive independently places the article in Volume 17, Issue 5.
  https://journals.aps.org/pr/issues/17/5
- National Academy of Sciences biographical bibliography for Bergen Davis lists
  the 1921 Davis & Stempel article at Phys. Rev. 17:608.
  https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/davis-bergen.pdf
- Later literature independently cites the same work, including a historical
  Bell System Technical Journal discussion of crystal rocking curves and a
  modern crystal-stress review using the exact DOI.

**Identity / later-use cross-check:** PASS.

**Trajectory / B cross-source validation:** NOT YET VALIDATED.

## Candidate W2006869700

**OpenAlex candidate:** Carl Eckart, *Some Studies Concerning Rotating Axes and
Polyatomic Molecules*.

Independent identity evidence:

- American Physical Society record: Carl Eckart, *Physical Review* **47**,
  552–558, published 1 April 1935, DOI `10.1103/PhysRev.47.552`.
  https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.47.552
- APS volume archive independently places the paper in Volume 47, Issue 7.
  https://journals.aps.org/pr/issues/47/7
- The work is repeatedly cited in later molecular/vibrational-dynamics
  literature. A Biophysical Journal article page exposes an independent Scopus
  count of 896 for the Eckart 1935 reference at the time represented by that
  page, providing strong evidence of substantial later recognition.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3495(02)75147-3
- Recent and historical articles continue to cite the exact DOI/title,
  including Journal of Chemical Physics and Physical Review sources.

**Identity / substantial later-use cross-check:** PASS.

**Trajectory / B cross-source validation:** NOT YET VALIDATED.

## Scientific interpretation

These checks reduce the risk that either provisional OpenAlex case is a
bibliographic identity artifact. They do **not** validate the OpenAlex annual
citation histories, awakening years, or Beauty Coefficients.

Therefore both papers remain provisional mechanism cases. They may be described
as:

> publisher-identity-crosschecked, with independent evidence of later
> scientific use, but with citation-trajectory/B validation still pending.

Do not upgrade either paper to a source-validated robust Sleeping Beauty until
an independent bibliographic source reproduces enough of the citation
trajectory to test the relevant gate.
