# ARIS4C017 · Agent takeover brief

## What this project is

**Predicting the Empty Cells of Human Language Space: A Falsification-First Test of Typological Constraint and Unrealized Configurations**

A conceptual successor to ARIS4C002 that shifts from testing one global circular geometry to testing whether competing typological constraint models can predict held-out languages, masked feature bundles, and calibrated support over unobserved regions of language space. Extinct-language claims are tertiary and require independently documented historical holdouts.
