# ARIS4C017 · LING-02 · Research context

## Core idea

Move beyond asking whether languages occupy one elegant global geometry. Ask whether empirically learned constraints can **predict what was hidden**.

The long-range motivation resembles a “periodic table” only in the predictive sense: a successful theory should forecast missing or not-yet-observed combinations. The project does not require periodicity, circularity, or one privileged geometry.

## Predecessor

ARIS4C002 / LING-01 is finished and found that the tested simple global circular organization was not supported by predictive evidence.

017 preserves that negative result and treats it as a design constraint.

## Key distinctions

- unobserved ≠ impossible;
- database-missing ≠ extinct;
- family-held-out generalization ≠ random cross-validation;
- predictive success ≠ proof of one true ontology;
- compatibility score ≠ historical existence claim.
