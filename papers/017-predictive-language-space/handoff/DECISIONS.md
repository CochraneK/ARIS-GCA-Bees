# ARIS4C017 · LING-02 · Decision log

## 2026-09-19 · Split from ARIS4C002

**Decision:** Create a new paper rather than extending 002.

**Why:** 002 tests whether observed typological structure has a simple global circular organization. 017 changes the prediction target to held-out/missing/unobserved configurations. The scientific question, target, and evaluation regime are materially different.

**Rejected alternative:** `2.1` or an in-place rewrite of 002. This would blur a completed falsification result with a new successor hypothesis.

## 2026-09-19 · Dual numbering

**Decision:** Use **ARIS4C-017 · LING-02**. Register 002 as **ARIS4C-002 · LING-01**.

**Why:** Global IDs remain stable across the whole portfolio, while thematic series IDs express lineage without renumbering old papers.

## 2026-09-19 · Claim boundary

**Decision:** Held-out observed languages/configurations are the primary validation target. Empty-space and extinct-language claims are downstream layers.

**Why:** Absence in modern typological databases is not evidence that a configuration is impossible or that a specific extinct language existed.
