# PREREGISTRATION AMENDMENT 003 — temporal persistence profile lock

**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Outcome status at amendment:** contemporary confirmatory country×discipline outcomes remain unopened.  
**Applies to:** `PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT.md` v0.1 + Amendments 001–002.

This amendment closes an implementation gap in RQ persistence. The previously
frozen pooled 2007–2022 models estimate an average cross-disciplinary gradient
across periods, but an average alone cannot distinguish decline, stability, or
reinforcement over time.

## A19. Secondary period-specific persistence profile

In addition to the three 2019–2022 headline coefficients and the pooled
2007–2022 specifications, estimate the identical within-period model separately
for each mature four-year window:

- 2007–2010;
- 2011–2014;
- 2015–2018;
- 2019–2022.

For each mature period report:

1. former-colony fractional-output Exposure × IKES;
2. former-colony Top-10% impact-rate Exposure × IKES;
3. former-colonial-tie dyadic ColonialTie × IKES.

The within-period country specifications use country and discipline fixed
effects. The within-period dyadic specifications use pair and the already
frozen endpoint×discipline×period fixed effects.

## A20. Recent output-only profile

Estimate the output model for **2023–2025** as a recent output-only sensitivity.

Do not estimate or interpret 2023–2025 as a mature Top-10% impact window.

## A21. Inferential status

These temporal coefficients are **secondary profile estimates**. They do not
expand the confirmatory family, which remains exactly:

1. 2019–2022 former-colony output;
2. 2019–2022 former-colony Top-10% impact;
3. 2019–2022 former-colonial-tie collaboration.

No period may be promoted to headline status based on effect size, sign, or
p-value.

The persistence profile is interpreted from the full prespecified coefficient
trajectory. A monotonic or non-monotonic pattern may be described, but no
post-hoc breakpoint is introduced without a dated post-outcome amendment.

## A22. Secondary-fit failure policy

A failed period-specific secondary fit is recorded as `FAILED_NO_REPAIR` with
its error. It is not silently dropped, and sample/model changes intended to
make that period converge require a dated amendment.

## A23. Relationship to pooled models

The pooled 2007–2022 models remain useful summaries. The new period-specific
profile does not replace them; it makes the time dimension of the persistence
question observable.

No contemporary outcome was inspected in making this amendment.
