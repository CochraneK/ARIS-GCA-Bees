## Figure 1. Chinese surname alphabetic population landscape

Population-weighted distribution of validated Chinese surname initials using ChineseNames 2025.8 counts and the pinned CCNC surname-specific Pinyin mapping. Panels show corrected A–Z population shares, cumulative mass across alphabetical rank, the corrected population-weighted mean rank (16.25491) relative to a uniform-A–Z benchmark (13.5), and the 99.9671% mapped versus 0.0329% excluded rare-surname population mass. This figure is descriptive and contains no scholarly-outcome effects.

## Figure 2. Measured alphabetical-authorship regimes

Chance-corrected alphabetical-authorship exposure across OpenAlex primary-topic fields and focal years, estimated from prior three-year article/conference-paper windows. All-author and 3+ author convention estimates are shown separately together with information support `D`.

## Figure 3. Preregistered within-work mechanism

Predicted normalized listed byline position across within-team relative alphabetical surname rank at prespecified low, median and high measured LOAO field exposure. The frozen work-fixed-effect interaction estimate is `beta2=0.64798` (SE 0.12289; 95% CI 0.40712–0.88884; two-sided p=1.34×10^-7). Larger normalized position denotes later placement in the byline.

## Figure 4. Mechanism robustness and falsification dashboard

Coefficient-and-95%-CI summary for the preregistered H1 and mandatory robustness/falsification analyses. The H1 interaction remains positive under focal-team 3+ authors (`beta=0.55599`), exposure estimated from 3+ author convention works (`beta=0.76303`), and both restrictions (`beta=0.65391`). In field-years at or below the chance-centered alphabetization benchmark (`ExcessAlpha<=0`), the surname-position slope is approximately zero (`beta=-0.00085`, p=0.902). Future-exposure and team-preserving permutation panels remain pending their canonical workflow artifact and will be displayed regardless of whether they support the mechanism.

## Figure 5. Secondary confirmatory results

Secondary confirmatory interaction estimates. H2 tests first-listed authorship with the same within-work mechanism and currently has raw `beta=-0.62801` (SE 0.14085; 95% CI -0.90407 to -0.35194; raw p=8.25×10^-6). H3 tests observed five-year publication persistence only if the frozen entry-cohort structural gate passes. H2 and H3 receive joint Holm adjustment. If H3 fails the preregistered adequacy gate, the panel will explicitly report that H3 was not confirmatorily estimable rather than substituting another endpoint.
