# ARIS4C006 · Research context

Last synchronized: 2026-09-19

## Project

**Alphabetical Exposure and Scholarly Credit in China's Research System**

## Core question

Within China's research system, does an author's relative alphabetical surname position become more predictive of listed byline position specifically when the field has a stronger independently measured prior convention of alphabetical authorship?

## Why the final design is not a simple “A beats Z” study

The project began from a broad Chinese name-letter/alphabetical-effect idea. The design was deliberately narrowed after novelty audits showed that:
- raw alphabetical surname-success associations already have substantial prior literature;
- Li & Yi already cover Chinese economists versus other Chinese fields/geographies;
- D'Angelo 2026 covers surname-initial bias in top-scientist/c-score selection;
- Crabtree–Holbein–Tsutsui directly occupy the country-of-origin surname-distribution inequality pivot;
- 2025–2026 work also occupies citation-system surname bias and generic long-run alphabetization exposure.

The defensible contribution became:

**Chinese population calibration × validated surname mapping × independently measured lagged local byline regime × within-work mechanism × locked longitudinal persistence extension.**

## Key empirical facts established before confirmatory analysis

- ChineseNames underlying surname population total: 1,181,719,774.
- Chinese surname initials are strongly non-uniform; uniform 1/26 is prohibited.
- Crossref structured family names are a high-confidence surname evidence route after positional reconciliation.
- Raw OpenAlex work-embedded author IDs can be stale/noncanonical; 61/61 sampled mismatches were repaired by current canonical author resolution.
- Raw source/journal-level exposure failed the prospectively frozen rank-reliability gate; primary exposure was conservatively narrowed to primary-topic field × prior-3-year window.
- All 26 current OpenAlex primary-topic fields passed the all-field feasibility gate.
- Historical coverage froze the focal window at 2011–2025.
- Within-author exposure variation passed the prospectively frozen gate.
- ChineseNames population counts plus pinned CCNC surname-specific Romanization became the final surname authority stack.
- The 2024-active longitudinal feasibility seed was explicitly forbidden as a confirmatory survivor-selected cohort.

## Claim discipline

A positive H1 supports an **institutional byline-order mechanism**:
- higher prior alphabetization exposure makes relative alphabetical surname position more predictive of actual byline position.

It does **not** show:
- surname letters cause intelligence/ability/personality;
- surname rank is randomized in China;
- CN affiliation means Chinese nationality/ethnicity;
- five-year bibliographic non-persistence is true academic exit;
- distal career effects are necessarily causal.
