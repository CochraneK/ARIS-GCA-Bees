# ARIS4C006 · Primary population and work-frame rule

Last updated: 2026-09-18

## Goal

Define a China-focused surname study without inferring citizenship, ethnicity, or ancestry from a name or institutional affiliation.

## Terminology

### CN-affiliated authorship

An authorship is `CN-affiliated` when the OpenAlex authorship contains at least one resolved institution with `country_code = CN`.

This is an affiliation-state definition only.

### ChineseNames-mappable surname

A validated family name is `ChineseNames-mappable` when it can be linked with high confidence to a surname represented in the pinned ChineseNames surname dictionary / reviewed Romanization mapping.

This means the **surname form** is in the study's Chinese surname frame. It does not establish the author's ethnicity, nationality, citizenship, birthplace, or self-identification.

## Primary work-level mechanism frame

An eligible focal work must:

1. have at least 2 total listed authors;
2. have a unique OpenAlex `primary_topic.field.id` in an eligible field;
3. have sufficient validated family-name evidence for **all listed authors** needed to compute whole-team alphabetical rank;
4. contain at least **2 CN-affiliated authorships with ChineseNames-mappable surnames**;
5. have a valid prior-3-year LOAO field exposure for each focal author under the frozen information threshold.

### Regression rows

Only the CN-affiliated, ChineseNames-mappable focal authorships are rows in the primary within-work regression.

A work therefore contributes to the primary work-fixed-effect estimand only when at least two focal authorships remain after all exclusions.

### Relative alphabetical rank

For each focal authorship, `RelAlphaRank` is calculated relative to the **entire actual author list**, not only the CN-affiliated or ChineseNames-mappable subset.

This preserves the real alphabetical pressure generated by all coauthors.

## Why this frame

It avoids two errors:

- equating a Chinese institution with Chinese identity;
- calling a person Chinese merely because their surname resembles a Chinese surname.

The scientific object is narrower and observable:

> the behavior of validated Chinese surname forms carried by CN-affiliated authorships inside the mainland-China scholarly system.

## Secondary frames

### Mixed-team structural analysis

Works with >=1 CN-affiliated ChineseNames-mappable focal authorship may enter secondary models that do not require a within-work comparison among two focal authors.

### Domestic-concentration robustness

A stricter robustness subset may require:
- all or a preregistered high share of resolved affiliations on the work to be CN;
- >=2 focal ChineseNames-mappable CN authorships.

### International-collaboration extension

Internationally mixed teams are retained as a secondary mechanism/heterogeneity analysis, not used to infer nationality or migration.

## Population calibration

ChineseNames population frequencies are used only for surnames that map to the ChineseNames dictionary.

Authors/surnames outside that dictionary:
- can help define the focal author's whole-team `RelAlphaRank` when their structured family name is reliable;
- cannot receive a Chinese population surname frequency;
- are not focal rows in the primary Chinese-surname mechanism model.

## Stop / narrow rule

If the final eligible work frame contains too few multi-focal-author works within a field/year context, that context cannot contribute to the work-fixed-effect primary model.

The project may then:
- retain that field for secondary structural models; or
- exclude it from the primary mechanism frame under a sample-size rule frozen before focal outcome estimation.

It may not relax the ChineseNames-mappable or surname-confidence requirement merely to increase sample size.
