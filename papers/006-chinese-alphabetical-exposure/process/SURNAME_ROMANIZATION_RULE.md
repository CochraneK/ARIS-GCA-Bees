# ARIS4C006 · Frozen surname romanization and focal-form rule

Last updated: 2026-09-19

## Authority split

The primary mapping deliberately separates **population frequency** from **surname pronunciation**.

- **ChineseNames 2025.8**: population counts/ppm, surname inventory, compound-surname flag.
- **Pinned CCNC surname lexicon**: surname-specific canonical Hanyu-Pinyin family form and alphabetic ordering key.
- **Crossref**: observed structured bibliographic `family`.
- **OpenAlex**: authorship order, affiliation, field, canonical author identity.
- **pypinyin**: engineering QA only; not confirmatory pronunciation authority.

CCNC is pinned at commit:

`a14520b9cc8bd6b251aeb4a7453ab1a45f23aa15`

## Why the earlier pypinyin map was superseded

Pilot 14 showed high engineering coverage but generic character pronunciation can misread surname-specific polyphonic characters.

Pilot 15 found **31** ChineseNames surnames whose CCNC surname-specific initial differs from the ChineseNames `initial`, representing about **0.4383%** of the ChineseNames population mass.

Examples:
- 覃 -> **Tan**
- 单 -> **Shan**
- 解 -> **Xie**
- 褚 -> **Zhu**
- 仇 -> **Qiu**
- 查 -> **Zha**
- 区 -> **Ou**
- 万俟 -> **Moqi**

Therefore the old pypinyin-based primary map is invalid for confirmatory pronunciation/order and is retained only for engineering provenance.

## Primary focal surname routes

### Route H — direct Han

A focal surname is Tier 1A only when:

1. Han family name is directly recoverable under the compound-surname-first parser;
2. exact Han surname occurs in ChineseNames;
3. exact Han surname also occurs in the pinned CCNC surname lexicon;
4. CCNC supplies the canonical surname-specific Romanized ordering key.

ChineseNames supplies population mass; CCNC supplies pronunciation/order.

### Route P — structured Romanized family

A focal surname is Tier 1B only when:

1. DOI-linked Crossref metadata supplies a structured `family`;
2. OpenAlex/Crossref author lists pass the frozen positional reconciliation rule;
3. normalized structured family exactly equals a frozen CCNC canonical surname form in the ChineseNames × CCNC intersection;
4. available ORCID/canonical-author evidence does not conflict.

Unreviewed historical/regional/legacy Romanizations are not primary-route matches.

Examples such as Lee, Chan, Wong, Cheung, Tsang, Hsü or Wade-Giles-style forms are not guessed into a ChineseNames surname.

## Dictionary coverage

Pilot 15:

- ChineseNames surname rows: **1,806**
- CCNC dictionary rows: **1,606**
- direct Han intersection: **1,166**
- row-count intersection: **64.56%**
- represented ChineseNames population: **99.9671%**
- excluded population mass: about **0.0329%**
- 2024 all-field exact bibliographic mapping coverage: **96.05%**

Primary analysis accepts this tiny population-mass loss in exchange for surname-specific pronunciation validity.

## Homophonous Romanized forms

When multiple Han surnames share one canonical Romanized form:

- population counts are summed over all compatible ChineseNames surnames;
- the shared Romanized form/initial is retained;
- a specific Han-character surname is not imputed unless independently observed.

Direct-Han records may retain exact-character population counts as a secondary descriptive attribute.

## Whole-team alphabetical rank

Only focal CN-affiliated ChineseNames-mappable surnames need population mapping.

All coauthors used to construct whole-team `RelAlphaRank` need a reliable structured family-name ordering key but do not need to map to ChineseNames.

Thus:
- Chinese population attributes attach only to focal mapped surnames;
- reliable non-focal family names still determine relative alphabetical position.

## Primary denominator

The primary Chinese surname population denominator is the **ChineseNames × pinned-CCNC direct Han intersection**, renormalized over the represented population mass.

The corrected A-Z baseline is generated by `code/17_ccnc_corrected_population_baseline.py`.

ChineseNames' original `initial` column is not used as confirmatory pronunciation truth.

## Parser/map version

Frozen primary mapping label after corrected-baseline materialization:

`aris4c006-surname-map-v2-ccnc`

Components:
- ChineseNames 2025.8 population counts;
- pinned CCNC surname-specific Romanization lexicon;
- Crossref structured-family reconciliation;
- direct-Han compound-first parsing;
- no unreviewed legacy/regional aliases;
- homophone population aggregation.

Any future alias/variant expansion increments the mapping version and remains secondary unless needed to correct a documented bug.
