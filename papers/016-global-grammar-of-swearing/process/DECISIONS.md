# ARIS4C016 Decisions

Updated: 2026-09-18

1. **Do not build a swear-word dictionary as the scientific endpoint.**
   The target is explanatory structure: taboo domains, pragmatics, form, genealogy and cultural variation.

2. **Start from an existing open dataset before expanding globally.**
   Sulpizio et al. (2024) is the Phase-0 anchor.

3. **Separate language from culture whenever design permits.**
   Same-language/different-country and multilingual-country contrasts receive special priority.

4. **Model genealogy.**
   Related languages are not independent observations.

5. **Use multi-label semantic coding.**
   A word can simultaneously be sexual, kinship-targeted, identity-targeted, etc.

6. **Treat translation as annotation, not equivalence.**
   Native-language form and community meaning remain primary.

7. **Keep public outputs research-safe.**
   The atlas can report categories, distributions and censored examples without turning the project into a browsable slur catalogue.

8. **No universal claims from exploratory Phase 0.**
   Universality requires unrelated-family replication and held-out tests.

9. **No causal culture claims from country correlations.**
   Cultural predictors remain association-level unless a stronger identification strategy is available.
