### Original research motivation retained

The originating question was whether birth timing and death timing are related,
with particular interest in whether any reproducible relationship could bear on
traditional calendrical / Bazi-style claims.

The canonical project intentionally uses a falsification-first interpretation:
a birth–death association by itself is **not** evidence for astrology/Bazi.
Ordinary seasonality, early-life environment, birthday/anniversary effects,
cultural calendars, coding artifacts, and matched pseudo-calendar controls must
be addressed first. Traditional-calendar/Bazi features are tested only as
prespecified incremental predictors with held-out and independent replication.

### Frozen interpretation ladder

The project keeps the following layers separate:

1. H1 — developmental / seasonal environment.
2. H2 — birthday / anniversary effects.
3. H3 — cultural-calendar effects.
4. H4 — traditional Chinese calendar / Bazi incremental prediction.

Evidence for H1–H3 must not be redescribed as support for H4. The strongest
traditional-calendar claim requires preregistered incremental predictive value,
matched pseudo-calendar controls, untouched holdouts, and independent
cross-context replication.
