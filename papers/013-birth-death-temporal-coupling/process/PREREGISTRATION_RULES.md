# Preregistration Rules · ARIS4C013

1. **Freeze the traditional feature dictionary before holdout access.** Every feature needs a deterministic algorithm, source, required precision, prediction status, outcomes, and multiplicity family.
2. **Keep calendar systems separate.** Gregorian date, meteorological season, solar terms, lunar date, sexagenary cycle, zodiac animal, Five Elements, and Four Pillars are not interchangeable.
3. **Full Bazi requires hour-of-birth.** Without hour, call the analysis partial traditional-calendar testing.
4. **Fit a strong ordinary baseline first.** Flexible seasonality and demographic predictors precede traditional features.
5. **Use matched-complexity controls.** Compare every traditional feature family against pseudo-systems with similar degrees of freedom.
6. **Protect the final holdout.** Evaluate the locked confirmatory specification once; later analyses are exploratory.
7. **Do not equate significance with prediction.** Require effect size, calibration, held-out gain, robustness, and replication.
8. **Cultural belief is a competing mechanism, not supernatural evidence.**
9. **Nulls are publishable.** Birthday or seasonal effects can replicate even if traditional features add nothing.
10. **Report the full frozen rule set and all confirmatory outcomes.**

## Holdout and effect-size addendum

11. **Use a ±1% practical-effect margin for the ordinary birthday-coupling primary effect.** O/E inside 0.99–1.01 is not promoted as substantively meaningful merely because it is statistically detectable.

12. **Treat 90% interval containment inside 0.99–1.01 as practical-null equivalence.**

13. **Require temporal stability for a replicated candidate H2 effect.** At least 7 of 9 holdout annual point estimates must be on the pooled side of 1.

14. **Do not open 1997–2005 until the mechanical two-step holdout release protocol validates.**

15. **Do not use H3/H4 traditional-calendar features to rescue a failed ordinary-calendar H2 result.** H3/H4 are separately frozen prediction families, not post-hoc replacements for the primary birthday analysis.

16. **Treat traditional date features as deterministic representations of DOB, not additional information beyond DOB.** Evidence is about reproducible structure/compression under equal-complexity comparisons.

17. **Do not use free categorical Gan-Zhi/zodiac fit as evidence for traditional semantics.** Such fits are invariant to relabeling.

18. **Separate feature freeze from outcome-model freeze.** Feature v1.0 may be materialized, but H3/H4 mortality analysis remains prohibited until the source-specific model schema is independently frozen.

19. **Traditional semantic comparisons require matched pseudo systems.** The frozen pseudo seed set is 13001–14000 inclusive (1,000 systems); outcome-dependent reseeding is prohibited.
