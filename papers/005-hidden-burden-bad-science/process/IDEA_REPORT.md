# IDEA REPORT — ARIS4C005

## Origin

Initial question:

> How can we estimate the total amount of scholarly publication worldwide, the amount affected by fraud or related problems, and the impact of those problems on the world?

The idea expanded through structured discussion before ARIS execution because an immediate “search → count retractions → extrapolate” workflow would have prematurely frozen an underdeveloped concept.

---

# 1. Initial naive formulation rejected

Rejected formulation:

> Count global papers, estimate a fraud percentage, multiply, then convert to money.

Reasons:

- researcher-level self-report prevalence is not article-level prevalence;
- retractions are detected/corrected cases, not the latent problem universe;
- retractions include honest error and procedural issues;
- global R&D spending is not a paper-production budget that can be multiplied by a fraud rate;
- publication ecosystems differ in detection/correction capacity;
- downstream harm is networked and cannot be represented by source-paper count alone.

---

# 2. First conceptual expansion

The problem was reframed as a chain:

```text
global scholarly output
  -> integrity / reliability state
  -> detection and correction
  -> citation and evidence propagation
  -> research resources and careers
  -> innovation / translation
```

This created four distinct quantities:

1. known/detected problems;
2. latent/estimated severe integrity failures;
3. broader unreliable/wasteful research;
4. downstream affected knowledge and decisions.

---

# 3. Human opportunity-cost expansion

A second discussion introduced harms that ordinary retraction bibliometrics miss.

## 3.1 Researcher youth / time

Converted into the measurable concept **Researcher-Life-Years (RLY)** rather than rhetorical “years of youth wasted.”

RLY is decomposed into:

- production effort;
- misdirected follow-up;
- avoidable replication/verification;
- investigation/correction/reanalysis.

## 3.2 Legitimate research crowded out

The original idea “how many good papers could have been published instead?” was revised because journal capacity is not universally fixed.

More defensible outcomes:

- delayed entry of alternatives;
- funding/attention displacement;
- reduced researcher entry;
- publication-flow changes in matched topics.

The primary innovation concept became **Innovation Delay Years**, not “one bad paper = one missing good paper.”

## 3.3 Sleeping Beauties

The user proposed estimating how many Sleeping Beauty papers were buried by bad science.

The idea was retained but decomposed:

1. observed delayed recognition;
2. awakening delay;
3. counterfactual permanent non-awakening.

The final concept **Never-Woken Sleeping Beauties** is explicitly exploratory and model-based.

---

# 4. Idea Saturation round

A deliberate final mining pass searched for first-order harms not yet represented.

New independent dimensions that emerged:

## 4.1 Participant sacrifice

Human research participants may bear time, burden, and risk while invalid/nonpublished work fails to enter usable knowledge.

This became **Participant Sacrifice Without Knowledge Gain**.

## 4.2 Innocent collaborator career spillovers

Research misconduct can impose citation/reputation/career penalties on uninvolved prior collaborators.

This became **Collateral Career Damage** rather than being hidden inside RLY.

## 4.3 Reviewer/editor/correction labor

Problematic science consumes scarce peer-review, editorial, investigation, and evidence-update capacity.

This became **Integrity Maintenance Debt**.

## 4.4 Persistence after formal correction

Retraction does not delete knowledge already embedded in downstream papers, reviews, or guidelines.

This produced two new dynamic concepts:

- **Knowledge Ghost Half-Life**;
- **Epistemic Reproduction Number (`R_E`)**.

---

# 5. Saturation decision

A subsequent literature pass mainly supplied evidence for existing categories instead of revealing another independent first-order loss family.

Therefore idea mining was declared sufficiently saturated and the loss ontology was frozen into five layers:

```text
L1 production resources
L2 labor / career capital
L3 epistemic / knowledge system
L4 innovation / opportunity
L5 societal / translation
```

Future ideas enter only if they cannot be represented as a child of these layers.

---

# 6. Frozen high-value concepts

### Primary / intended empirical targets

- global detected lower bound;
- calibrated latent severe-integrity prevalence;
- Researcher-Life-Years;
- Scientific Contamination Footprint;
- Knowledge Ghost Half-Life;
- Innovation Delay Years;
- attributable direct financial cost;
- participant sacrifice in a clinical subset;
- Integrity Maintenance Debt where measurable.

### Secondary

- Epistemic Reproduction Number;
- collateral career damage;
- Scientific Detour Years;
- funding/attention crowding-out.

### Exploratory

- Never-Woken Sleeping Beauties;
- Talent Misallocation;
- Trust Tax.

---

# 7. Narrative idea retained for eventual public visualization

The PuddingSkill-style story should progressively change the reader's denominator:

```text
How many papers exist?
        ↓
How many have been formally retracted?
        ↓
Retraction is detection, not prevalence.
        ↓
How much severe failure may remain hidden?
        ↓
How many downstream works depend on it?
        ↓
How much researcher/participant/editor time did it consume?
        ↓
How much did it delay legitimate alternatives?
        ↓
What valuable science might never have been recognized?
```

The intended final realization is not that every flawed paper is fraud, but that the largest burden may lie in **downstream opportunity cost that ordinary retraction counts cannot see**.

---

# 8. Rhetorical boundary

The eventual story may use vivid units such as “equivalent full research careers” or “PhD-length blocks of researcher time” only as transparent unit conversions from an empirical RLY estimate.

Such conversions must never be phrased as literal counts of people whose lives were destroyed.

Similarly, a Never-Woken Sleeping Beauty estimate is an expected counterfactual opportunity count, not proof that specific ignored papers would have become landmark discoveries.

---

# 9. Final idea-quality verdict

The project is no longer a generic research-integrity paper. Its distinctive contribution is the combination of:

- latent prevalence measurement;
- detection-process correction;
- semantic knowledge-propagation analysis;
- human-time accounting;
- causal innovation-delay analysis;
- delayed-recognition counterfactuals.

That combination is the frozen ARIS4C005 research identity.
