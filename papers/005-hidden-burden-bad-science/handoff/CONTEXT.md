# ARIS4C005 · Research context

## Project

**The Hidden Burden of Bad Science: Estimating the Global Scale and Downstream Cost of Research Integrity Failures**

## Compact scope

A global, uncertainty-aware meta-research program with a completed public-data denominator/correction pilot and a real probability-traceable 1,339-work Pilot B seed frame; it separates confirmed misconduct, severe scientific reliability failures, publication-process failures and broader research waste, then estimates prevalence and downstream burden only after detector calibration and independent adjudication.
