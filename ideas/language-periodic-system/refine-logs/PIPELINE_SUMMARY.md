## Main empirical results

### Stage 0
Real TLI structure was substantially more compressible than a marginal-preserving shuffle null, justifying further modeling.

### Stage 1
A simple circular model underperformed tree/graph alternatives, including under top-level-family hold-out.

### Stage 1B
A fairer directly optimized circle produced a mixed result: competitive at 40 features, weaker at 60. This prevented an overconfident early rejection.

### Stage 1C
Using TLI-defined domains and complete valid family-held-out replicates, no predefined domain met the joint predictive/stability criterion for local periodicity. Grammar linear order showed a highly stable circular ordering but tree/low-rank models predicted better.

## Current working interpretation

The evidence supports a distinction between:

- **stable ordering / recurrent structure**, which clearly exists in some domains; and
- **periodic geometry**, which has not emerged as the best predictive representation.

The likely manuscript identity is therefore:

> **Beyond the Periodic Table: Predictive Geometry of Cross-Linguistic Structural Space**

rather than a claim to have discovered a language periodic table.
