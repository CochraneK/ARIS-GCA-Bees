Testing the periodic-table hypothesis of human language

A predictive stress test of a simple global circular organization of linguistic features across TLI, GBI and WALS. The tested global circle is not supported by held-out prediction and direct circularity diagnostics; this does not establish a universal tree geometry.

Interpretation boundary

What this paper supportsThe simple single-global-circle form tested here is not supported. Hierarchical and other non-circular benchmarks generalize better under the current representations. This is not evidence that every form of linguistic periodicity is impossible, nor that language has one universal tree geometry.
